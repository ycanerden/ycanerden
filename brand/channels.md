## LinkedIn playbook

1. Open on a room, a number, or a decision.
2. One story. One implication.
3. Soft ask: come to a night, steal the tool, reply with your city.
4. Post morning Brussels time (07:30–09:00) Tue–Thu when possible.
5. First 30 minutes: reply to every comment as a human.

**Formats that fit Can**

- Event recap (same night or next morning)
- "We were tired of hearing X, so we built Y"
- System teardown (how two people run four cities)
- Career scar with a present-tense lesson

## X playbook

1. **Replies first.** 15 minutes on other people's posts in: AI coding, European founders, Cursor, GTM, community.
2. **Ship logs.** What you built today in one screenshot's worth of words.
3. **Split LinkedIn.** One LinkedIn post becomes 2–3 X posts, not a paste.
4. **Quote with a point.** Never "this."
5. **Pin** a Habitat or bunny post, not a bio essay.

**Formats**

- Observation (1–2 lines)
- Mini-story (4 lines)
- Tool drop with a URL
- Reply that could stand alone as a post

## Cross-posting rule

Same raw material. Different sentences. Different ask.

LinkedIn ask = come / host / talk.
X ask = try the tool / reply with your stack / quote.
