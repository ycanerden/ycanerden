# Can Erden, Inc.

A one-person company. The product is **distribution**. Habitat, tools, and GTM work are the proof.

## Why treat a person as a company

Companies have:

- A category they own
- A weekly operating cadence
- Channel-specific packaging
- A pipeline (attention → trust → conversations → outcomes)
- A ship log, not a mood

Hobby posting waits for inspiration. A company ships on a schedule.

## North star

Own the sentence:

> The person in Europe who turns AI into shipped companies — in one night, and in the systems around it.

1. **Habitat** — come to a night, host a city, join the community
2. **GTM systems** — Can builds pipelines with AI; people hire / partner / follow for that
3. **Tools** — habitat-bunny, mesh, skills. Steal the playbook.

## Scoreboard

Track weekly, not vibely:

- Posts published (X / LinkedIn)
- Replies / quote-posts that started conversations
- Profile visits and inbound (Habitat, GTM, tools)
- Events announced or recapped
- Ships turned into posts (target: 100%)

LinkedIn already has reach. X is the underbuilt channel. Treat X like a new market launch.
