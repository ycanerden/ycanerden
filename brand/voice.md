# Voice

Write like Can after a Habitat night: tired, specific, slightly impatient with excuses.

## Sound

- Short sentences. Then one longer one if it earns it.
- Concrete nouns: cities, clocks, URLs, MVPs, partners.
- Numbers when they are real. No fake precision.
- First person. "We" for Habitat. "I" for personal GTM and tools.
- Dry humor. No punchline-forced memes.

## Words we use

ship, night, room, clock, hop, demo, live URL, excuse, founder, builder, pipeline, system, steal this

## Words we do not use

synergy, unlock your potential, game-changer, just shipped this crazy thing, 10x your, thought leadership, humbled, delighte, revolutionize, the future of work is here

## Patterns that work

**Cold open with the tension**

> Most startup events start with pitching. We start with building.

**Proof before advice**

> 200 MVPs. Then the lesson.

**Anti-excuse**

> "I need a co-founder" is not a strategy. Sit next to someone for four hours.

**Show the container**

> A prompt is a suggestion. The bunny is a gate.

## Patterns that fail

- Listicles with no lived scene
- "5 things I learned from..." unless the fifth thing is a scar
- Announcing tools without a user in the sentence
- Humble-brag ambassador flex with no scene
- Corporate Donna updates

## Channel texture

**LinkedIn:** one scene, one lesson, one ask. 120–220 words. Line breaks. No hashtag walls. One link max.

**X:** one idea per post. 1–4 lines. Threads only when the story needs beats. Replies > original posts for growth. No "thread 🧵👇" energy.

## Signature close (use rarely)

Always one hop ahead.

## Language

English by default. Dutch or Turkish only when the audience is that room.
