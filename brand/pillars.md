# Content pillars

Rotate these. Do not post the same pillar two days in a row unless an event forces it.

## 1. The night (Habitat)

Scenes from rooms: formation, the clock, the demo, the person who arrived with nothing.

**Job:** make people want to come or host.

**Examples:** recap, city announcement, "what happened in the last 15 minutes," host playbook crumbs.

## 2. The container (tools)

habitat-bunny, mesh, skills. How you force shipping when the agent is too agreeable.

**Job:** builders steal the tool, then associate it with Can / Habitat.

## 3. The system (GTM)

How you grow a community, a pipeline, or a product with AI. Process, not theory.

**Job:** inbound for GTM conversations. Proof you operate like a company.

**Guardrail:** no Donna internals, no customer names, no unreleased metrics.

## 4. The scar (career)

Rapider, Gobo, Koç, Vlerick, moving between Turkey and Belgium, building with two people.

**Job:** trust. You've done the unglamorous work.

## 5. The room (network)

Cursor, Lovable, Vercel, European founders, ambassadors. People, not logos.

**Job:** borrowed distribution. Tag people who were actually there.
