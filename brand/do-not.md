# Do not

This repo is public. Automations can see email and calendar. That does not make those things public.

## Never publish

- Donna customers, pipeline, revenue, unreleased features, internal Slack
- Other people's private messages
- Calendar leftovers (student cards, personal pickups, private 1:1s)
- Unannounced Habitat cities or partnerships
- Anything marked private, confidential, or "don't post"

## Never do this as an agent

- Send email to anyone except a **draft** to ycanerden@gmail.com
- Auto-post to X without `APPROVED` or `PUBLISH_MODE=on`
- Auto-comment on LinkedIn
- Follow/unfollow schemes, engagement pods, fake screenshots
- Tag people who were not in the room
- Invent testimonials
- Use Can's ambassador status as a flex with no scene
- Give legal, medical, or investment advice
- Argue politics

## Taste kills

- "Excited to announce"
- Emoji storms
- Hashtag soup
- Carousel-of-platitudes energy
- "As a founder and Cursor Ambassador..."
- AI-looking em-dash essays with no room in them

If it could be posted by any growth agency, delete it.
